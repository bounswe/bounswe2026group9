1"""Event recommendation notifications. 

2 

3When a host publishes a public event, find users whose past attendance pattern 

4(category overlap on `going` status of `ended` events) matches the new event, 

5and emit `event_recommended` notifications to them. 

6 

7Privacy / safety guarantees: 

8 - Private events never produce recommendations 

9 - Cancelled / ended / draft / updated events never trigger this emitter 

10 - The host of the event is never recommended their own event 

11 - Users who already bookmarked or are going on the new event are skipped 

12 (they will get the regular update channel instead) 

13 - Per-user daily cap (default 3) prevents notification fatigue 

14"""
